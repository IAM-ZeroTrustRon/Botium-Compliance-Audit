# Botium Toys – Internal IT Security & Compliance Audit

## 📋 Executive Summary
An internal security audit and risk assessment conducted for the fictional retail and e-commerce company Botium Toys. This project applies the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF) to evaluate the organization's security posture, identify critical vulnerabilities, and assess compliance with PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC 1/SOC 2 standards.

The audit revealed significant gaps in access control, encryption, incident response readiness, and regulatory compliance. These findings highlight the need for immediate remediation to reduce risk to customer data, payment systems, and business continuity.

## 🎯 Audit Scope & Goals
* **Business Continuity:** Evaluate resilience of IT systems supporting e-commerce operations.
* **Regulatory Compliance:** Assess alignment with PCI DSS (payment security) and GDPR (EU data privacy).
* **Asset Management:** Inventory and evaluate IT-managed systems, physical assets, and legacy infrastructure.

## 🛠️ Security Skills Demonstrated
* NIST CSF Implementation
* IT Asset Inventory & Risk Assessment
* Compliance Auditing (PCI DSS, GDPR, SOC)
* Administrative, Technical & Physical Control Evaluation
* Vulnerability Identification & Mitigation Strategy
* Risk Heat Mapping & Diagramming

## 📊 Asset Inventory & Risk Assessment
**Assets Evaluated:**
* On-premise servers
* Employee workstations
* POS systems
* Customer databases
* Legacy systems requiring manual monitoring

**Key Findings:**
* No encryption for customer or payment data
* No password policies or password management system
* No disaster recovery plan
* No IDS or centralized logging
* PCI DSS: All critical controls marked No
* GDPR: Most privacy controls marked No

## 🛡️ Controls & Compliance Checklist
* **Controls Assessment:** 11 of 15 controls marked No
* **PCI DSS:** 4 of 4 best practices marked No
* **GDPR:** 3 of 4 best practices marked No
* **SOC 1/SOC 2:** 3 of 4 best practices marked No
